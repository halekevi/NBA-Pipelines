How these grades work

Each leg: Line + side · Actual (box-score stat; — if none exists yet, e.g. rainout/postponed) · Edge (model edge, not the result). Graded exports: outputs/2026-07-21/graded_*.xlsx. Hit rate = paid ÷ (paid + no payout) among fully graded tickets (all-void slips excluded). Sheets with Flex in the title (3+ legs) use flex cash rules: at least n−1 hits and at most one miss. UNGRADED (3 legs): the matched graded row still has no actual or HIT/MISS—often unfinished games, voids, or grading before final stats. Re-run the grader after games; on hosts without outputs/2026-07-21/, commit workbooks under ui_runner/graded_slate/2026-07-21/ before rebuilding this page.
